About Me

I hold an MSc in Cyber Security from the University of Essex and have extensive experience managing and improving enterprise technology environments. In my role supporting the Parliament Management Commission (PMC), I oversee the management and reliability of critical IT systems while identifying, assessing, and implementing new technologies to enhance operational efficiency. My work involves troubleshooting and optimizing computer systems, collaborating with stakeholders to deliver and evaluate technology projects, and providing training and technical support to staff. I have also designed systems to monitor technology reliability and availability, implemented structured end-user feedback and error reporting mechanisms, and continuously assess systems against established data security standards. Through process refinement and service improvement, I focus on strengthening both the quality and resilience of IT services.
